CrowdMind — AI-Orchestrated Interactive Live Experiences

Proposal:

CrowdMind envisions an AI-powered framework that transforms live events into adaptive, audience-responsive experiences. Building upon crowd-driven visual generation, the system continuously interprets multimodal audience interactions—such as text, emojis, gestures, applause, movement, and real-time engagement signals—to understand the collective state of the audience.

Using this evolving representation, CrowdMind dynamically orchestrates multiple elements of the live experience, including stage visuals, lighting effects, animations, and other immersive media, enabling performances to adapt organically to audience participation. Rather than generating a single output, the framework focuses on continuous experience orchestration, where AI acts as a real-time creative collaborator between performers and the audience.

The long-term research goal is to investigate multimodal fusion, streaming generative AI, real-time conditioning, and interactive experience design for next-generation live entertainment.
